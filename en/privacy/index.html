Privacy

Development preview. An App Store download is not yet available. Updated September 7, 2026.

No account or cross-app tracking

This version has no account, cloud sync, developer-operated data collection server, advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK.

Location access

With permission, the app uses your current location to find nearby places, set the starting point and follow a route while the app is open. It requests only access while in use and does not provide background navigation. You can change or disable access in system settings. Without permission, you can still plan a route by choosing start and destination manually; automatic location updates are unavailable.

Map requests use the internet

Place suggestions, search, directions, nearby points of interest and map snapshots use Apple MapKit. Depending on the request, search terms, search areas, start and destination coordinates and travel mode are sent to the map service; processing is not entirely on this device. Apple and regional providers process data under their own privacy policies. The developer does not receive these requests through a separate server.

Saved route and progress

The app saves your most recent route and stop progress on this device, including the start, destination and route coordinates, so you can continue after reopening. A new route replaces the previous one. You can clear it from the home screen or route menu. This file is excluded from system backups and is not sent to the developer. Your travel-mode preference is saved on the device. The system and map service may manage their own caches.

Sharing and opening other apps

A route image is passed to another app, service or save location only when you choose to share it. Images may include your start, destination and private places. Ending a route or uninstalling the app does not delete copies saved in Photos, Files or by recipients. Manage those copies separately.

“Navigate with Apple Maps” passes the start, destination and travel mode to Apple Maps. Further processing follows Apple Maps settings and policies.

Support email and website

When you contact the developer, your email service transmits your email address, message and attachments. The developer uses them to reply and investigate your issue. Avoid sending private addresses or full travel histories. Contact the support email if you need help with information you have sent.

The website has no analytics scripts, ads, external fonts or tracking cookies. It is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process access logs such as IP addresses.

Updates and contact

If future versions add accounts, additional storage, cloud services or analytics, this notice will be updated before release. For privacy questions, contact zhouwei@linux.com.
