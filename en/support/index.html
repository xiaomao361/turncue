Support

Development preview. An App Store download is not yet available. Updated September 7, 2026.

Cannot get your current location?

Check system Location Services and TurnCue’s permission to use location while the app is open. Try again in an open area. Reduced accuracy or a weak signal may affect the starting point and stop detection. Open location settings from About & Help.

Cannot find a place or generate a route?

Add a city, road or building name and check the address and distance. Try a wider search or choose on the map. Check your connection, cancel and retry, or change the destination or travel mode. TurnCue cannot supply routes unavailable from the map service.

Cycling directions unavailable?

Cycling depends on local map coverage. Do not use walking or driving directions as cycling instructions.

Will navigation continue if I go off route or lock the screen?

There is no rerouting, background navigation or lock-screen guidance. Choose “Navigate with Apple Maps” when you need full navigation.

Cannot find a landmark, or a stop advanced early?

Landmarks come from map points of interest and may not be visible. Check the surrounding roads from a safe place. Pause or go back one stop if needed, or open full navigation.

Why is the final section not complete?

There may still be some distance to go on the final section. Confirm arrival after you actually reach the destination.

Sharing failed, or the image has no live progress?

Map snapshots need a connection. Reconnect and try again if sharing fails. The image is static: it has no live location or shared progress. Canceling the share sheet does not send the image.

How do I clear my data?

Clear the saved route from the home screen, or end and clear it from the route menu. Planning a new route replaces the previous record. Delete saved images separately in Photos or Files. Recipients and their services manage images you have sent.

What should I include in feedback?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, travel mode, steps to reproduce, and expected and actual results. For map coverage issues, a city or public landmark is enough. Hide private addresses and precise starting points in screenshots.
