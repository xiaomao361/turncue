Guide

Development preview. An App Store download is not yet available. Updated September 7, 2026.

1. Find your destination

Allow location access while using the app, then enter a place and check its address and distance. Add a city, road or full building name to narrow your search. You can also choose both start and destination on the map to plan ahead without location permission.

2. Choose how to travel

After selecting a destination, choose walking, driving or cycling. The initial mode is suggested by distance. You can cancel search or route generation; editing the query discards older requests.

3. Get to know the route

The map shows the full route and highlights a few key points. Tap a map marker or stop in the list to see how to get there and what to do next. Nearby places are references only; visibility has not been verified. Complex maneuvers keep the original map instructions. An overall compass direction is not an immediate turn instruction.

4. Follow while the app is open

Choose “Start following” to see the overall direction, current target and action on arrival. Walking stops switch only after consecutive accurate location updates. Driving and cycling stops require manual confirmation; stop safely before using the app. Returning to the overview pauses following and keeps progress. Choose “Continue following” to resume. Pause at the bottom of the follow screen, or go back one stop from its menu. Entering the final section does not mean you have arrived. Confirm completion when you actually reach the destination.

5. Save or share

Choose “Save or share route image” from the overview menu to create a static image with the map, path, destination and stop notes. Then choose where to save or share it in the system share sheet. The image does not update with your position. Check for private places before sharing.

When you need full navigation

“Navigate with Apple Maps” passes the start, destination and travel mode. Apple Maps may calculate a different route based on current conditions.

What to expect

Search, routes and map snapshots need an internet connection. There is no rerouting, background navigation, spoken turn-by-turn guidance or lock-screen guidance. Landmarks may not be visible; times and distances are estimates. Follow road signs and local conditions. Stop safely before using the app while driving or cycling.
